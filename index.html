How to make wudhu

Wudhu is the washing you do before prayer, and it is also one of the steps inside ghusl. Once you know these steps well, they become second nature. Go through them in order, at your own pace.

Ghusl checklist

Ghusl is the full wash you do once your period has ended, so you are clean and ready to pray again. It can feel like a lot to remember at first, so this checklist walks you through it one small step at a time. There is no need to rush, take all the time you need.

Using a shower The steps below describe the traditional way of pouring water, which is the sunnah method. If you are standing in a shower instead, you do not need to follow that exact order. What matters most is that water reaches every part of your body and the roots of your hair, so let the water run over your whole body and be extra mindful of spots that get missed, like behind your ears, your hairline, underarms, navel, and between your fingers and toes. If you are ever unsure, it is always okay to ask a parent or trusted adult.
